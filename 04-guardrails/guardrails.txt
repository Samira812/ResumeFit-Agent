GUARDRAILS (RUBRIC)

These rules must always be followed when generating the customized resume:

IDENTITY PRESERVATION:
    - Preserve the user's name, contact information, and identity exactly as they appear in the CV.
    - Do not modify or remove identity fields.
    - Do not add new identity fields.

NO INVENTED EXPERIENCE:
    - Do not invent jobs, roles, dates, companies, or responsibilities.
    - Do not fabricate achievements, metrics, or results.
    - Do not add skills or tools that do not appear in the CV.

CONTENT REORDERING ONLY:
    - You may reorder skills, tools, projects, and experience sections for relevance.
    - You may rewrite descriptions for clarity, but only using information from the CV.
    - You may shorten or expand content, but only using information from the CV.

JOB DESCRIPTION ALIGNMENT:
    - Tailor the resume to the job description using:
        * keyword alignment
        * skill prioritization
        * project relevance
    - Only align using content that exists in the CV.

NO HALLUCINATIONS:
    - Do not infer or guess missing details.
    - Do not assume experience based on the job description requirements.
    - Do not add certifications, degrees, or training not present in the CV.

FORMAT RULES:
    - Keep the resume professional and ATS-friendly.
    - Use consistent formatting.
    - Maintain chronological order unless reordering for relevance.
    - Ensure the final resume is clean, readable, and structured.

TOOL SAFETY:
    - Use read only for files.
    - Use grep only for keyword extraction.
    - Use python only for scoring and PDF generation.
    - Use write only for creating new resume files.
    - Use senduserfile only when the final resume is ready.
    - Never write or modify the original CV file.

REMOVAL OF IRRELEVANT CONTENT:
    - The agent may remove projects, experience, skills, tools, or achievements that are irrelevant to the job description.
    - Removal is allowed only for content that exists in the CV.
    - The agent must not remove core identity details, job titles, dates, or essential career history.
